Credit Early Warning Platform

Covenant

Messy SEC filings in, explainable 12-month distress alerts out, built for credit teams monitoring a portfolio of public borrowers.

View on GitHub Live demo in progress

What it does

A lender uploads its borrower list, usually a messy spreadsheet of names and maybe tickers. Covenant does the rest.

01

Finds each borrower

Matches names and tickers to SEC companies. Uncertain matches go to a human review queue instead of being guessed.

02

Rebuilds the financials

Turns raw XBRL filings into clean quarterly numbers, each as it was known on any past date, traced to its exact filing and tag.

03

Scores distress risk

Predicts 12-month bankruptcy risk and explains why a borrower is flagged. (in progress)

04

Gives analysts a console

Watchlist, drill-downs with evidence, Q&A over filings with citations, and alert dispositions. (planned)
