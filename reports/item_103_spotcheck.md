## What this means for labels

- One bankruptcy usually produces several Item 1.03 8-Ks (petition, then plan confirmation or emergence), and some companies go bankrupt more than once.
- Filing dates alone cannot separate the two: Hertz emerged 13 months after its petition, while Spirit filed a second petition about 9 months after its first. Telling petitions from emergence filings needs the 8-K text (open question for Phase 4 labels).
- As-of dates between a petition and emergence must not be scored as 'healthy'; the company is already in bankruptcy.
